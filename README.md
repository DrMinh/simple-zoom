## ✨ The Feature

**Simple Zoom does one thing — and does it well:**

> **Let you inspect images with precise, smooth, pixel-perfect zooming.**

Drop an image into the page, paste one from your clipboard, or open a local image — then freely **zoom, pan, and inspect every pixel**.

It is designed to feel more like a dedicated image viewer than a browser's default image page.

---

## 🔍 Pixel-Perfect Zoom

Zooming in doesn't turn your image into a blurry mess.

At **100%**, one image pixel represents one screen pixel.

At higher zoom levels, individual pixels remain clearly visible:

```text
100%   → 1 image pixel = 1 screen pixel
200%   → 1 image pixel = 2 × 2 screen pixels
500%   → 1 image pixel = 5 × 5 screen pixels
```

This makes Simple Zoom particularly useful for:

* Pixel art
* Game assets
* UI graphics
* Sprites
* Icons
* Small details
* Image inspection

---

## 🖱️ Zoom Where You Point

The mouse wheel doesn't simply zoom toward the center.

**Zoom happens around your cursor.**

Place your cursor over an interesting detail and zoom in — that detail stays under your cursor.

This makes exploring large images much more natural.

---

## 🖐️ Pan Freely

When zoomed in, simply **drag the image** to move around it.

No complicated controls.

**Drag → Move**

**Wheel → Zoom**

That's it.

---

## 📋 Paste From Clipboard

You don't even need to save an image first.

Copy an image and press:

**`Ctrl + V`**

Simple Zoom reads the image directly from your clipboard and displays it immediately.
